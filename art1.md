## 3. VOD (Video on Demand) Library Quality

A premium service should offer much more than just live television. Evaluate the Video on Demand (VOD) library for recent movies, complete TV series, and updated content.

## 10. Multi-Device Usage (Concurrent Connections)

Consider how many people in your household will be watching at the same time. Many basic plans only allow for a single connection. If you try to watch on your living room TV and your bedroom TV simultaneously on a single-connection plan, your stream will freeze or your account may be banned. Ensure the provider offers multi-room or multi-connection upgrades if you need them.

## 11. 4K Availability and Bandwidth Requirements

As 4K televisions become the standard in every home, 4K IPTV streams are becoming a heavily requested feature. Be aware that true 4K streaming requires significantly more bandwidth (usually a minimum of 25-50 Mbps of stable internet speed). Ensure the provider explicitly offers 4K options and that your home network is capable of handling the load.
